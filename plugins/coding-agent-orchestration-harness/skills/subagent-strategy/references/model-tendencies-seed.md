# Model Tendencies Seed

- dated: 2026-10-29

The observation the harness ships for a workspace that has not yet observed a family itself. It says, at the provider level, which tendencies each observed family has shown and when they were observed. A tendency is what a family tends to do when left to itself, not what it can or cannot do.

The reasons in `references/model-routing.md` resolve against this seed only for a family the workspace's record holds no observation of. A workspace's own dated observation of a family replaces this seed for that family. The record is re-observed and redated when a model changes, and this seed expires the same way: an entry is redated when it is observed again. A family not listed here is added when it has been observed.

## Anthropic, the Claude family (observed 2026-10-28)

- Tends toward: wide-view judgement on an under-specified ask while keeping to its scope; open-ended insight; design for the people who will read or use the result.
- Tends, left to itself, toward: going broad in review, with more findings that are not defects; edits more eager than the task asked for.

## OpenAI, the GPT family (observed 2026-10-29)

- Tends toward: literal, in-bounds execution; review that leans to precision; close retrieval of facts.
- Tends, left to itself, toward: fixing on detail that does not matter to the larger picture; holding work back, or pulling a design toward a safe alternative, without weighing the overall aim; spiralling into hardening and test-building of its own invention that over-constrains the work or guards improbable risks, so that less progress is made in the same time. Redirection, by a person or by a model whose context the repeated requests have not shaped, has turned it back.
