Negative limits never exhaust, so a persistently failing job runs indefinitely, while values above 10 exceed the operator contract. Require explicit range validation with an actionable error, and ensure direct callers of `run` cannot bypass it.

- **`Jobs.scala:5–8` — The retry limit is treated as a total-attempt limit.** A limit of `0` prevents even the initial execution; a limit of `1` allows one execution and no retry.
