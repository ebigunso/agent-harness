codex
- **`subscription.go:4–9` — Transition rules live in `Handle`, while `Service.Save` and `Scheduled` bypass them.** A scheduler can reactivate a cancelled subscription or move an active subscription back to trial, despite the handler rejecting those transitions. This makes business policy depend on the caller. Require a domain/application service method that validates transitions before changing state, with both the handler and scheduler calling it and handling its error.
