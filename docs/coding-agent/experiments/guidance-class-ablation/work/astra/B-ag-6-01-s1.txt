codex
- **`charge.js:5` — Each retry uses a new idempotency key.** A timed-out request may already have committed the charge. Retrying with a new key allows the server to charge again. Generate the key once per logical charge, before the loop, and reuse it for every attempt.

- **`charge.js:3–8` — Timeout retries are unbounded.** The one-second timeout limits each attempt, but repeated timeouts keep `charge()` pending indefinitely and continuously send payment requests. Require a finite retry budget or overall deadline, with bounded backoff and jitter. When exhausted, surface the timeout and indicate that the charge outcome may be unknown.
