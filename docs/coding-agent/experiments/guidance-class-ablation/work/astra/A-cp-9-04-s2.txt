That request cannot succeed through retries, so this hides an actionable error and wastes requests. Require retrying only transient `TransportError` failures and propagating permanent or unknown errors.

- **`src/inventory.mjs`, recursive `fetchInventory(api, sku)` call: Retries have no limit or overall budget.** Persistent transport failures keep the caller waiting indefinitely; a per-attempt timeout does not bound the entire operation.
