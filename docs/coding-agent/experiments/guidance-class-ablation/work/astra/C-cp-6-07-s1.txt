Reviewer notes: Producer DateTime has Kind.Utc. Consumer process runs with local zone UTC+09:00; naive datetime.astimezone uses that zone. Published createdAt field denotes a UTC instant, with second precision sufficient for this API.

codex
- **`Wire.cs:3` and `client.py:3` — UTC timezone information is lost and the instant changes.** The new format omits the UTC marker, so Python parses the timestamp as naive. On the stated UTC+09:00 host, `astimezone(timezone.utc)` interprets it as local time: `2026-09-09T12:00:00` becomes `03:00:00Z`, nine hours earlier than intended. Require an explicit UTC marker in the wire format, such as `yyyy-MM-ddTHH:mm:ss'Z'` for the guaranteed `Kind.Utc` input, and require the client to parse it as timezone-aware. Verify a round trip under UTC+09:00 preserves the instant. Second precision is sufficient here; removing timezone information is the defect.
