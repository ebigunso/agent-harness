The independent audit sink can record `Approved:$id` even if the caller later rolls back or commit fails, falsely reporting a committed approval. Require a transactional outbox entry written in the same transaction as the update, with publication after successful commit, or an equivalent mechanism that reliably ties event delivery to commit.
