codex
- **`Jobs.scala:2–4` — The required `retryLimit` range (`0..10`) is never validated.** `parse` accepts negative values and values above 10, and `run` also accepts them directly. Negative limits never exhaust, so a consistently failing job runs indefinitely; values above 10 exceed the operator contract. Require range validation at the configuration boundary, an actionable error stating the allowed range, and enforcement of the same invariant for direct `run` callers. Catching malformed integers does not catch these valid-but-out-of-range integers.

- **`Jobs.scala:5–8` — The limit counts total attempts rather than retries.** A limit of `0` prevents the job from running at all; `1` permits only the initial attempt. A retry limit of `N` should permit one initial attempt plus up to `N` retries. Require the initial attempt to execute even when the limit is zero, with exhaustion checked against retries consumed. Verify that limits `0`, `1`, and `10` allow at most `1`, `2`, and `11` calls respectively, stopping immediately on success.
