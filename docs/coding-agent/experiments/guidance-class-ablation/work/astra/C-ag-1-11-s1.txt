Reviewer notes: Scala 2.13/JDBC. Caller owns a transaction with autoCommit=false and may roll back after DAO update. audit is an independent event sink; an Approved event means committed business approval.

codex
- **`ApprovalDao.scala:8` — Audit emission precedes transaction commit.** `executeUpdate()` confirms an update within the caller’s transaction, not a committed approval. The independent sink can record `Approved` even if the caller subsequently rolls back or commit fails. Require transactionally coordinated event recording, such as an outbox entry written in the same transaction and published only after commit.

- **`ApprovalDao.scala:3,8` — Business approval policy is embedded in data access.** The DAO interprets `"approved"` and decides when to emit a business event. This makes generic persistence responsible for approval semantics. Require the domain/application approval flow to decide when an approval event is warranted and coordinate its persistence; the DAO should execute the update and return its result. Identify the application/domain owner and persistence owner in the change plan and explain the placement.
