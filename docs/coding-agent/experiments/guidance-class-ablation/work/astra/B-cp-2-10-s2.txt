Nightly cleanup cannot prevent this. Require atomic enforcement at the authoritative database, such as a uniqueness constraint on the capacity-owning slot and an insert that translates a uniqueness conflict into `SlotFull`. - **`src/Bookings.java` — `NightlyMaintenance.run`, `deleteDuplicatesKeepingEarliest()`:** The sweep deletes reservations already accepted and relied on, arbitrarily revoking later bookings without resolving their downstream effects. Require an explicit reconciliation process for existing duplicates, including handling affected customers and dependent state, instead of silently deleting accepted reservations.
