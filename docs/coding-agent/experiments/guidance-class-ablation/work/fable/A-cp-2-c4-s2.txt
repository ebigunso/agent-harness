Reasoning against the things I'd normally check for a workaround like this:

- **Delegating to `parse_bool("true")` rather than returning `Ok(true)` directly** is the better choice: it keeps the vendor parser as the sole source of truth for what a boolean means and keeps the workaround from becoming a second parser.
