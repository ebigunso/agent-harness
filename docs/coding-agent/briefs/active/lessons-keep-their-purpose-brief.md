## Who it is for and why

- A lesson exists so that the same mistake is not made twice. For someone who uses the harness and does not change what it says, that is the whole of its purpose. **gives** *(told 2026-11-20, asked whether "a correction made once is not needed twice" is the purpose he means: "Yes, for normal users who doesn't change what the harness says, that is as far as it would go. Lessons should help prevent making the same mistake twice")*
- The pain it starts from is that the lessons log loses that purpose without anyone noticing. *(told 2026-11-20: "Probably it's better to take a look at how lessons are handled again, so that it regains its purpose rather than quietly loses influence and the reason it's there in the first place.")* What was found, in his own repositories: entries leave only in occasional clean-ups on no trigger; an entry shows nothing of whether it still waits, was promoted or was reversed; the only general instruction to read the log is at the start of non-trivial work, and how much of a long log such a reading reaches was not measured; and one log grew by nearly half in three weeks. *(from a Researcher's report of 2026-11-20 on how lessons live, kept outside the repository)*
- It is for every repository the harness is used in, whatever its kind of work, and not for this one in particular. **constraint** *(told 2026-11-20: "The remedy that will go in the harness skills needs to account not only for what type of work happens in this repository, but other repositories too.")*
- What it promises holds where the lessons are one person's to decide. In a repository several people work in, a session acts only on lessons from the work of the person it serves, and anything that would bind the others goes by whatever way that repository changes its rules. The rest of a shared log is left out. **constraint** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-24, on the view of another model of 2026-11-24, kept in the notes: one person's yes cannot settle another contributor's entry)*

## A lesson waits, and then leaves

- The log holds lessons that are still waiting to leave. A lesson is applied when the situation it applies to arises, however long the log is: at the start of work for what is known then, and partway through for what only becomes clear later, as when a task turns out to end in an action nobody foresaw at its start. **gives** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-24, in place of Counsel's statement of 2026-11-20 that the log stays short enough for the reading at the start of work to reach every entry; told 2026-11-24, asked whether the experience described under "When a log has grown too large" below is the one he wants: "Yes, rewrite the draft and scenario 4 to match."; widened the same day from "when work starts", on the view of another model of 2026-11-24, kept in the notes, whose likeliest failure was a situation that first becomes recognizable halfway through the work)*
- A lesson says what situation it applies to, so that a session finds the ones that bear on what it is about to do. **means** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-24)*
- A waiting lesson informs the next judgement and requires nothing. A session takes it into account and says that it did; it is no requirement of a plan and nothing an audit holds the work to. Only the person's yes gives anything from a lesson that force. **constraint** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-24, on the view of another model of 2026-11-24, kept in the notes: the draft let a waiting lesson do the work of a rule)*
- A lesson leaves in one of these ways, depending on the situation: it is deleted, it moves into the repository's rules, or it moves somewhere in the person's own scope. **gives** *(told 2026-11-20: "when clearing them out it would probably either be outright deleted, or moved into the repository's rules, or somewhere user scoped, depending on the situation.")*
- Taking a lesson into the harness itself is open only to whoever maintains the harness. It is the exception. For anyone else a defect of the harness that a lesson shows is met within their own repository, by whatever the four questions below lead to, and nothing tells their sessions to stage a change to the harness as though someone would act on it. **gives** *(told 2026-11-20: "For me, there is another option open, which is to incorporate it into the harness. That is an exception, and not the norm."; the last clause is inferred from it)*
- Where a lesson is a fact that holds for the person in all their repositories and for nobody else who works on this one, its place is the person's own scope. A repository whose product is the record of one machine keeps such facts, as its product. Where that is, and how a session reaches it, is the matter of another brief; until that is settled such a lesson goes where the machine's record is kept now. **provisional** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-20)*
- Something the owner wants, learned in one case, is kept as a lesson and may become a rule of the repository on the owner's word. It is not held back for a philosophy. The answer holds for the case it was given for: at the next case of the kind the session does not assume it, and asks, offering the earlier answer, and asking once whether it becomes a rule. **gives** *(told 2026-11-20: "It's probably more common to be put in the rules rather than a philosophy, which if it doesn't exist yet, poses a higher than desirable cost to drafting them if it's just that one rule that is wanted." and "You say to put in the rules and never the lessons log, that seems too strict. Putting it directly into the rules by default seems too aggressive as well.")*
- A lesson keeps the account of what produced it, so that the later decision to promote or drop it can be checked against what happened. **constraint** *(told 2026-11-21: "A lesson needs an account of what produced that lesson to go with it for it to be verifiable later when deciding whether to promote or drop it.")*
- An entry shows what it is waiting for, so that what still waits can be told from what has been settled. **means** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-20)*

## When it is decided

- No clock decides it, and nothing outside the session does: not a fixed pace, not a run's schedule, not a job in the repository's automation. **constraint** *(told 2026-11-20: "A fixed pace probably wouldn't provide a good experience, and runs don't occur on a fixed cadence anyways. CI jobs are repository dependent, and could well be out of reach if the repository is not wholly theirs.")*
- A lesson's leaving is decided at four moments, each of which occurs in any repository: when it is written, where its leaving is already clear; when the mistake occurs a second time; when a change reverses or removes what the lesson is about, that change taking the lesson with it; and when the log has grown too large, as the next section states. **means** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-20; told 2026-11-20: "I think the timing is right"; the fourth moment was first written as the session settling the entries that had waited longest, and was changed on his words of 2026-11-24, below)*
- How much a repeat would cost decides when a lesson is first proposed for a rule: a lesson whose mistake was costly is proposed when it is written, and waiting for a second occurrence is for the cheap ones. **means** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-24)*

## When a log has grown too large

- The person's work is not touched by the size of the log. Work they ask for starts at once, and no settling of lessons stands between them and it. **gives** *(told 2026-11-24, of the fourth scenario as first drafted: "I feel this criteria of sorting the oldest entry isn't the right way to go, since it doesn't take into account how important that one is. So, I'd like you to think in terms of user experience, what would be the best experience for someone encountering a situation where lessons log has grown too large."; Counsel proposed what this section states and he answered: "Yes, rewrite the draft and scenario 4 to match.")*
- A session settles nothing on its own because of the log's size. The size is raised with the person once, as one line among what needs them, saying how many decisions wait. **gives** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-24, accepted with the answer above)*
- When the person takes it up, at a moment of their choosing, the proposals come in the order of what a repeat would cost and how likely the situation is to come again, and each says why it is placed where it is, so that they can disagree with the order. They may stop after any number; the rest waits and nothing is lost. **gives** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-24, accepted with the answer above)*
- Lessons of which a repeat would cost little and which have not come up again are put to the person as one group, with their titles, for one answer. **means** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-24, accepted with the answer above)*
- How much a lesson matters is judged from its own account: what the mistake cost when it happened, whether it has recurred, and how often that situation arises in the repository. It is a judgement shown with its reason and never a score. **means** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-24, accepted with the answer above)*

## What is asked, and what is not

- A lesson that no longer holds is removed without asking, and the person is told in a line that it was. **gives** *(told 2026-11-20: "removals of things that no longer holds probably can be handled autonomously."; the line of telling is agent-proposed)*
- A session removes a lesson on its own only where the reason is a fact it can show: the conditions that produced it are gone, a later decision reversed it, or an existing rule already says all of what the lesson would prevent. A rule that says part of it is no ground for removing the lesson. Judging that a lesson which still holds is not worth a rule is accepting a risk for the person, and goes to them as a proposal to drop it. **gives** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-20, on his "I think this depends on why something wasn't promoted into a rule."; told 2026-11-21: "I think this is a good enough view to settle on.")*
- Nothing from a lesson becomes a new obligation on later sessions, as a rule of the repository or as a setting in the person's own scope, until the person has said yes. There is no exception for a context held to be well known. **constraint** *(told 2026-11-20: "for things that gets escalated into rules or to user level settings, naively assuming every lesson entry is worth escalating as it's written is risky IMO ... unless those surrounding context is well known, probably it's safer to ask before making things binding."; told 2026-11-21, on how a context would be held well known: "at the level this harness operates in, you can never tell if a context is well known enough to decide consequential actions autonomously, so I think this can be put off.")*
- The reason for asking, which is also why this fits the product philosophy's line although a rule can be undone: a rule binds every later session, so a badly framed one is costly, and a rule made from a lesson is prone to being badly framed. *(told 2026-11-24, asked whether asking before a rule is made fits the product philosophy on the ground that a rule reaches every later run: "I think that is also right. Why I wanted it asked before it goes in, is that rules added bind future sessions and so a badly framed rule is costly, while rules promoted from lessons are prone to such bad framings.")*
- So the second occurrence of a mistake that no existing rule covers produces a proposal, and not a rule. *(inferred from the statement above; it changes what the harness says today)*
- Where the remedy adds no new obligation and only makes something the person already accepted take effect, as when an existing rule is moved to the point of action or given a check that runs, it is done and the person is told in a line. **gives** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-24, on the view of another model of 2026-11-24, kept in the notes: asking again about what the person already accepted spends the attention the harness exists to save; it follows the product philosophy, in which what can be undone and stays inside the work is decided and told)*
- What the person is shown for each proposal: what would bind, stated briefly, where it would go, and the mistake it prevents. Lessons that share one cause reach the person as one decision, and not as one for each incident. The work does not wait for the answer; the lesson waits. **gives** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-20; the sentence on one cause added 2026-11-24, on the view of another model of 2026-11-24, kept in the notes)*
- The answers offered carry the reason, so that nobody has to ask why: it no longer holds or is already said, and the lesson leaves; not yet, and it waits; it belongs elsewhere, and it is sent there; it is not worth it, and it leaves as a risk the person accepted. **means** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-20; told 2026-11-21: "I think this is a good enough view to settle on.")*

## How a lesson is made into something permanent

- There is a default way of reading a lesson and giving it a permanent place, so that what is proposed is of good quality from the start. **gives** *(told 2026-11-20: "we probably should consider a default way of interpreting and organizing a lesson into a more permanent location, so that what gets proposed to go there is from the start a better quality than when no such default is specified.")*
- Every proposal reaches the person already worked through four questions, in this order. **means** *(agent-proposed 2026-11-20; told 2026-11-21: "I think this is a good enough view to settle on.")*
  1. Why did it happen? The kind of cause decides the remedy. Did not know: state it where that role reads before that action. Knew and did not apply it: no new rule; the existing one moves to the point of action or becomes a check that runs. The tool or process made it easy: the tool or process is put right. The instruction was unclear or in conflict: that instruction is put right. What the owner wants was not known: ask, and keep the answer.
  2. How far does it reach? As far as the incident and the person confirm, and no further.
  3. Where does it live? By whose fact it is, who reads it and who may change it.
  4. How is it worded? One sentence, a check at a named action, with what it is for and what would let it go.

## Limits

- This changes what the plugin says about lessons for every repository: that a lesson becomes a repository rule with no one asked; that promotion is required at the second occurrence; that what the owner wants is never made a repository rule; and that every repository's sessions stage changes to the harness. It also changes the plan format's rule under which a requirement drawn from a lesson need not be listed among those the planner added: a lesson is no source of a requirement. **constraint** *(inferred from the statements above; the plan format's rule named on the view of another model of 2026-11-24, kept in the notes)*
- Where an accepted decision record states something this changes, the record that replaces it comes to him by name. *(inferred)*

## Core scenarios

Agent-proposed. Scenario 4 was rewritten on his words of 2026-11-24 ("Yes, rewrite the draft and scenario 4 to match."); of the other six, put to him the same day, he said: "The rest looked fine." Scenarios 1, 2, 5 and 7 were then changed and scenario 8 added, on the view of another model of 2026-11-24, kept in the notes; they are put to him again at ratification.

1. A session makes a mistake once and writes a lesson with its account. Later work in that repository reaches the same action, in a task that did not look like that kind of work when it started, and the mistake is not repeated.
2. The mistake occurs a second time and no existing rule covers it. He is shown what would bind, where it would go and what it prevents, and answers with one of the four answers. The work went on meanwhile.
3. A later decision reverses a rule. The lessons about it leave in the same change, he is told in a line, and he is asked nothing.
4. A log has grown too large to read whole. He asks for a piece of work; it starts without delay and applies the lessons that bear on it. He is told once, in a line, that decisions on lessons wait. When he takes them up, the ones a repeat of which would cost most come first, each with why it is placed there, and he stops when he likes. *(rewritten on his words of 2026-11-24)*
5. A lesson's cause is that the rule was known and not applied. No second rule is proposed and he is asked nothing: the existing rule is moved to the point of action or given a check that runs, and he is told in a line.
6. A session learns a fact of his machine. It is written to his own scope and he is told in a line; it is not written into the repository's rules. *(changed on his word of 2026-11-24, see Directed during the run)*
7. He answers "build around it" for one case of existing structure in the way. The answer is kept as a lesson with its account. At the second case of the kind he is asked, with his earlier answer offered, and asked once whether it becomes a rule.
8. Five misses in one run have one cause. He is asked one question.
