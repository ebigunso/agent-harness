## Goal
- In any repository the plugin is used in, a session applies a lesson when the situation it applies to arises, a lesson leaves the log at one of four moments, and nothing from a lesson becomes a new obligation before the person's yes, as the governing brief states.

## Definition of Done
- The package validators and smoke tests pass (the commands of `docs/coding-agent/rules/common.md`, Repository-Specific Validation Commands).
- The plugin's text nowhere has a lesson made binding with no one asked, nowhere bars what the owner wants from the repository's rules, and tells no repository to stage changes to the harness.
- `plan-format` rule 12 and the plan-review snippet no longer treat a lesson as a source of a requirement.
- The entry form, the lookup, the four moments, the asking rules, the four questions and the handling of a log grown too large are stated once, in one reference of the `improvement-loop` skill, and pointed to from each point of action.

## Design
- Chosen: the lesson's entry is the only record of its state, one entry per cause, found by reading a short log whole or searching a long one by its heading and situation lines, at the start of work and again at any action not in view at the last lookup; whatever is in the log still waits, and a settled entry leaves in the change that settles it. Structure: one owner per piece of state (the entry; no index, no proposals file); the handling is one `improvement-loop` reference with one-line pointers from the Orchestrator's skills, `rulebook`, `git-workflow` and the role adapters. Evolution: the staging machinery and two references are deleted; legacy free-form entries are found by heading and never migrated; the next change to the handling edits one file. Verification: validators keep contract checks only; behaviour is shown by trials in throwaway repositories against a control on the current text. Operation: one read of a short log or one search of a long one per lookup, the search growing by one line per entry; no cost to the person from the log's size. Human: labelled prose lines a person can read and search; no parser. Safety: a waiting lesson sits in no rule file, so it cannot be read as a rule; a rule from a lesson is written only with the person's quoted yes. (consumers: see the Compatibility stance)
- Alternative: push instead of look up: when a lesson is written, a one-line pointer to it is placed in the rule file the affected role already reads, under a heading saying it binds nothing; the log keeps only accounts. Structure: state in two places. Evolution: the pile moves into the rule files. Verification: no lookup to show, but no reach to an action unforeseen at the start. Operation: every session pays for every waiting lesson on every run. Human: no lookup step to remember. Safety: a waiting lesson in a rule file is read as a rule.
- Why chosen: the brief's constraint is that a waiting lesson informs and requires nothing, and the repeat it starts from was a rule already in the file, missed at an action nobody foresaw; pushing reaches neither. Fit: `docs/engineering-philosophy.md` (one thing in one place) and ADR-D-0026's rejection of candidate sections beside live policy.
