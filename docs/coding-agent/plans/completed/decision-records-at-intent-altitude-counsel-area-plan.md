## Goal
- The eight active records of the area "Counsel and the value discussion" (ADR-D-0034, 0036, 0044, 0045, 0046, 0047, 0059, 0060) are checked against the record standard as it stands on main, and each decision that fails the check has a new complete record proposed for it. No decision changes.

## Definition of Done
- Each of the eight records has its check's result ready for the run record's table: meets, rewritten (to which), split (into which), or raised.
- Each new record is in the template's present form, states one decision by the owner's test, and names in `consulted` the models talked with, where that is recorded, and the model that wrote it.
- Every Decision clause of an old record has one of three fates, listed clause by clause: carried by a new record; held by a named accepted record that the new record's More Information names; or, only for what the brief lists as mechanics (routes, moves, file names, admission of a relay, template fill-ins), left to the plugin's text, quoted by file and line.
- These reach the owner by name: a record split in two or more; a record the run cannot show to keep its decision; a record whose revisit condition says something the old record's reason does not. Every other rewrite waits for the closeout audit's confirmation, record by record, that its decision is unchanged.
- The package validator passes.

## Planner-added requirements
- `consulted` carries the old record's entries as well as the model that wrote the new record. Needed because: the brief asks for the models talked with, and the old record's entries are the only record of who was consulted on the decision when it was made.
- Each task's report lists every Decision clause of the old record and where it went. Needed because: the brief's rule that a clause not carried is held by a named accepted record cannot be reviewed or audited without the list.
- A new record's file name, once numbered, is at most 109 characters. Needed because: a retired record's name gains 26 characters and names beyond 135 have failed the smoke test's copy of the tree (`docs/coding-agent/lessons.md`, the entry on file-name length).

## Scope / Non-goals
- Scope: new proposed records under `docs/coding-agent-orchestration-harness/decisions/`, and the run record's State line brought up to date for the merge of pull request 111.
- Non-goals:
  - Changing any decision's outcome; a decision found stale or contradicted is brought to the owner through Counsel and not rewritten.
  - The plugin's text, the record standard and its template.
  - Marking any record accepted, retiring any old record, and repairing references to it: these follow acceptance, as a small change of the run, as in the goal-mode area.
  - Rewriting ADR-D-0047 or ADR-D-0060 for their older form alone (Decision Log).
  - The two other areas not started.

## Design
- Chosen: one task per old record or closely tied pair, each writing the new records for it; no alternative required. The change touches no responsibility, contract, persisted state, new component, trust boundary or hot path.
