## What the text rests on

The philosophy is written in Counsel's wording, on his direction of 2026-11-11 that his ideas be taken as the base and not quoted. The ideas that are his:

- That the harness assumes people will not spend effort they do not have to, and aims to reduce what it asks of them.
- Who it is for: someone who does not know the mechanics; building for them also spares the person who does from decisions that could be cut.
- That finer settings exist as overrides to defaults, made on the person's direction or proposed by the agent, and applied automatically or put to the person depending on impact. The philosophy says only that a choice the person cannot judge is made for them; how overrides work is left to a decision record.
- The order of failures: building the wrong thing confidently; spending the person's attention on what could have been settled; deciding what was the person's, the last weaker than the other two.
- The line: a choice made for the person, told afterwards and easily changed, is acceptable; something that cannot be undone or that reaches outside, done without asking, is not.
- That waiting and parallelism are a gain in performance and not part of the core philosophy.
- That cost means money, is best handled automatically, and is tuned with care because a miss disappoints greatly.
- That the behaviours in section 2 read as examples of the time of writing and do not pin how the harness works.
