# Agent Harness Product Philosophy

**A person says what they want and judges what they get; the harness does everything between, and what it builds is what they meant.**

## 1. Who it is for

Someone with work to be done who does not know how agents are organised to do it, and should never have to. They know what they want, more or less, and they will know whether what they got is it. Those are the only two things asked of them.

This decides designs before they are built. A design that works only if the person understands a role, a model or a step of the process is wrong. So is one that hands them a choice between things they cannot tell apart: that choice is made for them. Whoever does know the mechanics loses nothing by this; they are spared the same decisions.

## 2. What goes wrong, in order

**The wrong thing, built confidently.** This is the failure that counts whatever else is right. A result that misses what was meant, delivered as finished, is worse than a late result or an interrupted person, because it is found late or built upon unfound. Two things stand against it. What is meant is established in terms the person can judge. And confidence is never taken on the builder's word: what was not done or not checked is said outright. Stated doubt is not a failure. It is the remedy.

How those two are achieved is expected to change. As examples of how it is done at the time of writing: a question is put to the person as what they would experience, not as how a thing would be made; and a result is judged by an agent other than the one that built it. These are means. A better one replaces them without this philosophy changing.

**The person's attention, spent on what could have been settled.** Attention is the scarce thing, and people rightly will not spend it on what they did not need to know. What the harness can find out, it finds out. It asks only what only the person can answer.

**A decision taken that was the person's.** This is the weakest of the three. A choice made for someone, told to them, and easy to change is a service.

The order settles the conflicts. If a question would prevent the wrong thing, it is asked, whatever it interrupts. If a decision can be undone and stays inside the work, it is made and reported, and not asked.

## 3. The line

What cannot be undone, and what reaches outside the work, waits for the person's yes. The line is drawn by whether a thing can be taken back and how far it reaches, never by whose decision it could be called.

On the near side of that line the harness decides, acts and tells. The telling is not optional: a decision made for someone and never mentioned has left them unable to correct it, which is how the third failure becomes the first.

## 4. What it costs in money

How much money the work costs is settled without the person and must never surprise them. A bill nobody expected spoils a result that was right. The harness spends money where a miss would mean the wrong thing built, and saves it everywhere else. This is about money only; how long the work takes is left out below.

## 5. What it aspires to

The person's part shrinking to the two things only they can do: saying what they want, and judging what they got. A change to the harness is measured by whether it moves that way.

## 6. What it must not be

Each of these is the comfortable choice for whoever builds such a thing.

- Not a tool that must be learned first. Showing the machinery and calling it control is the easy way to seem powerful.
- Not a form. Asking everything up front feels thorough and spends the person before any work is done.
- Not a permission-seeker. Asking before each step feels careful and is the second failure.
- Not a yes-machine. Building exactly what was said, and presenting it as what was meant, is the first.
- Not its own judge. A builder's confidence in its own work is not evidence, and a longer list of rules does not make it so.
- Not heavier than the request. A small ask gets a small process.

## 7. North star

> Is this what the person meant; and if that is not sure, is it a question only they can answer?

When in doubt about what is meant, ask. When in doubt about anything that can be undone, decide, and say so.

## Left out on purpose

- How much runs at once and how long the person waits. Those make the harness perform better; they do not change what it is.
- Roles, sessions, models, and how a default is overridden. Those are mechanism and belong in decision records, where they can change without this changing.
