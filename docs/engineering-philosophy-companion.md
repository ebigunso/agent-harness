## What the text rests on

The philosophy is written in Counsel's wording, on his direction of 2026-11-11 that his ideas be taken as the base and not quoted. The ideas that are his:

- The base: the engineering philosophy he ratified for another repository of his, with what was found during the work in this repository added.
- That the philosophy is about how the quality of the repository's contents is held.
- The root: intent clear enough, when a piece is read in a void, that anyone can later work on it with confidence in what they are working with.
- That clear is measured for the reader: content a person must read and judge is plain; complexity where only agents read and write is fine if it is the right shape; what confuses its own reader is a fault.
- That contents replaced while their reason stands are a fault whether or not anyone reads the replacements, and that a rule nobody can explain is much worse than a reasoned decision replaced because the world changed.
- The two concepts of the base he holds to: striving for clean code, and the document hierarchy.
- That clean has a second sense, elegance: what is needed with minimal complexity, and a design with a degree of extensibility built in so that it does not need an overhaul each time something new comes up.
- That those free to refactor would find no need or want to overhaul, kept in section 8 as the sign and not the aim.
- That a philosophy is a place for aspirations, including ones whose bearing on a decision is not apparent when written, kept because they describe the thought process.
